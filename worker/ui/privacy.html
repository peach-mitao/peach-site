统计偏好 / Analytics preferences

官网和演示站使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 统计页面访问，并以自有统计分析按钮点击、可见区域和匿名访问动线。为关联两站内的同次访问，会使用共享于 peach.video 子域名的随机会话 Cookie；连续 30 分钟没有活动后失效。行为明细保留 30 天；页面参考截图随对应数据清理。汇总统计保留三个月。

自有统计不记录输入框内容、搜索词、影片标题、完整访问网址、IP 或完整浏览器标识，不录制页面与操作视频。关闭页面后的去向无法观察；离开位置依据会话结束前最后记录的位置判断。

Both sites use Cloudflare Web Analytics and first-party click and journey analytics. A random session cookie shared across peach.video subdomains expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Detailed events are retained for 30 days; reference screenshots are removed when their associated data expires. Aggregate analytics are retained for three months. First-party analytics do not collect input text, search terms, movie titles, full URLs, IP addresses or full user-agent strings. No session recordings are made.
